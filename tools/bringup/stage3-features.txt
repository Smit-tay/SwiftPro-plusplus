//SwiftPro++ bring-up, stage 3: features. *** THIS FILE MOVES THE ARM. ***
//Run with: tools/bringup/run.sh tools/bringup/stage3-features.txt
//Clear a 300 mm radius around the base and keep a hand on the power switch.
//The arm reboots when the script opens the port; it must be in the probe pose (about B90 L46 R66).
#1 P2200
#2 M2203 N0
#3 M2203 N1
#4 M2203 N2
//ask Check: #1 about B90 L46 R66, and #2-#4 all "ok V1" (position read at boot, drivers on). If not, press Ctrl-C.

//0. Feed hold: base 90 -> 130 at F20 (about 1.6 s), held 0.4 s in. Expect $12 ok V1 (moving, held)
& #10 G2202 N0 V130 F20
//wait 0.4
& #11 S1000 V0
//wait 0.3
#12 M2200
//ask The base should be standing still now (held). Press Enter to resume.
& #13 S1000 V1
//wait 2.5
#14 P2200
#15 G2202 N0 V90 F20
#16 P2200

//1. To the acceptance test pose (B90 L70 R40, about X240 Z64): L up first, then R down
#20 G2202 N1 V70 F100
#21 G2202 N2 V40 F100
#22 P2200
#23 P2220

//2. Linear moves (G1): a 40 mm square at the test height, F50. The effector should travel straight edges
#30 G1 X260 Y20 Z64 F50
#31 P2220
#32 G1 X260 Y-20 Z64 F50
#33 P2220
#34 G1 X220 Y-20 Z64 F50
#35 P2220
#36 G1 X220 Y20 Z64 F50
#37 P2220
#38 G1 X240 Y0 Z64 F50
#39 P2220

//3. Streamed moves (M2124 V1): five replies at once, the arm runs the square WITHOUT STOPPING at the corners,
//then "@9 V0" when it stops
//ask Watch the corners of the next square. Press Enter to start.
#40 M2122 V1
#41 M2124 V1
#42 G0 X260 Y20 Z64 F100
#43 G0 X260 Y-20 Z64 F100
#44 G0 X220 Y-20 Z64 F100
#45 G0 X220 Y20 Z64 F100
#46 G0 X240 Y0 Z64 F100
//wait 6
#47 M2124 V0
#48 M2122 V0
//ask Did it run through the corners without stopping? Note it for the report. Press Enter to go on.

//4. Wrist: 45, 135, 90 deg
#50 G2202 N3 V45
//wait 1
#51 G2202 N3 V135
//wait 1
#52 G2202 N3 V90
#53 P2206 N3
//ask If a pump or a gripper is fitted, the next steps switch it on for 1 s each. Press Enter.
#54 M2231 V1
//wait 1
#55 P2231
#56 M2231 V0
#57 M2232 V1
//wait 1
#58 P2232
#59 M2232 V0

//5. Release and re-lock L (Q-6)
#60 P2200
//ask SUPPORT THE ARM with one hand: L is released next and the upper arm may drop. Press Enter when ready.
#61 M2202 N1
//ask Move L a few degrees by hand and hold it there. Press Enter: L is locked again and re-read.
#62 M2201 N1
#63 P2200
#64 M2120 V0.2
//wait 0.5
#65 M2121
//ask L is holding again; you can let go. Press Enter.

//6. Lost-step check (optional): the base turns slowly 90 -> 110 at F20. Hold it gently still with your hand;
//expect "@8 N0 V..." and "$70 E25", and the arm stops. To skip, just don't touch it
//ask Ready? Press Enter to start the base move.
#70 G2202 N0 V110 F20
#71 P2200
#72 G2202 N0 V90 F20
#73 P2200

//7. Back to the probe pose: R up first, then L down
#80 G2202 N2 V66 F100
#81 G2202 N1 V46 F100
#82 P2200
