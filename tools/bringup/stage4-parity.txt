//SwiftPro++ bring-up, stage 4: the V4.10.0 parity items A1, A2, A4, A5 (docs/PARITY.md). *** THIS FILE MOVES THE ARM. ***
//Run with: tools/bringup/run.sh tools/bringup/stage4-parity.txt
//Clear a 300 mm radius around the base and keep a hand on the power switch.
//The arm reboots when the script opens the port; it must be in the probe pose (about B90 L46 R66).
#1 P2200
#2 M2203 N0
#3 M2203 N1
#4 M2203 N2
//ask Check: #1 about B90 L46 R66, and #2-#4 all "ok V1". If not, press Ctrl-C.

//1. E27 (Q-70): with the base released, a move of L is refused and nothing moves
#10 M2202 N0
#11 G2202 N1 V50 F20
//ask Check: "$11 E27" and L did not move. Press Enter to lock the base again.
#12 M2201 N0
#13 P2200

//2. "!" and "~" (Q-71): base 90 -> 130 at F20 (about 1.6 s), held 0.4 s in, then resumed
& #20 G2202 N0 V130 F20
//wait 0.4
//raw !
//wait 0.5
#21 M2200
//ask Check: "ok @9 V0" right after the "!", #21 "ok V1", and the base standing still. Press Enter to resume.
//raw ~
//wait 2.5
#22 P2200
#23 G2202 N0 V90 F20

//3. Ctrl-X (Q-71): stop during a slow base move. Expect "ok" at once, then "$30 E25" after the brake and the re-sync
& #30 G2202 N0 V130 F20
//wait 0.5
//raw ^X
//wait 1
#31 P2200
#32 M2120 V0.2
//wait 0.5
#33 M2121
//ask Check: "ok" right after Ctrl-X, then "$30 E25"; #31 and the @3 B, L, AR fields agree (B = #31's B - 90). Press Enter.
#34 G2202 N0 V90 F20

//4. M2124 (Q-72), optional, needs your hand: with M2124 V1, a lost-step stop beeps when the re-sync corrects the base
//(more than 0.5 deg). Hold the base still by hand while it tries to move; to skip, just don't touch it
#40 M2124 V1
//ask Get ready to hold the base. Press Enter, then hold it for about a second.
#41 G2202 N0 V110 F20
//ask Check: "@8 N0 V...", "$41 E25" and a short beep (0.26 s) after the arm stopped. Press Enter.
#42 M2124 V0
#43 G2202 N0 V90 F20
#44 P2200

//5. Jogs (A4, Q-76). Each jog would run to 1 deg inside the limits, which are now your measured stops minus 1 deg
//(Q-83), so 2 deg inside the stops. Still, every jog here is cut short by Ctrl-X after 0.5-0.8 s, in a direction that
//keeps the effector at or above its height: a full jog to an edge is a separate, later test.
& #50 G2208 N0 F20
//wait 0.5
//raw ^X
//wait 1
#51 P2200
//ask Check: the base turned about 13 deg towards 180, "ok" after Ctrl-X, "$50 E25". Press Enter.
//The linear jog first finds its edge and checks the path, about 0.5 s on the AVR before it moves, so 0.8 s here
#56 P2220
& #52 G2207 N2 F20
//wait 0.8
//raw ^X
//wait 1
#53 P2220
//ask Check: the effector rose about 10 mm straight up (#53 against #56: X, Y about the same), "$52 E25". Press Enter.
#54 G2209
//ask Check: "$54 ok" and nothing moved (every joint is inside its limits). Press Enter.
#55 G2206 B90 L46 R66 F20

//6. P2236 (A4, Q-76): the base buttons' pins, V0 = pressed
#60 P2236 N0
#61 P2236 N1
//ask Check: #60 and #61 "ok V1". Now hold the Play button down and press Enter.
#62 P2236 N1
//ask Check: #62 "ok V0" (if V1, the Play pin differs on your board: report it). Release the button, press Enter.

//7. Read only (A7, A9 step 1; Q-80, Q-81): the external EEPROM, the AVR's own EEPROM, G2203. Nothing moves or is written
#70 M2211 N1 A0 T1
#71 M2211 N1 A65000 T1
#72 M2211 N0 A900 T1
#73 G2203
//ask Check: #70/#71 "ok V<n>" means the board has the external EEPROM (E26: no chip answered; report it). #72 is your work mode. #73 "ok". Press Enter.
